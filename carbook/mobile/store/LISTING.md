Carbook: Garage & Community

## Short description (80 characters maximum)
Track your garage, share car stories and discover community meetups.

## Full description
Good cars. Better company.

Carbook brings your car's logbook and your automotive community into one place.

YOUR GARAGE, ORGANIZED
Keep service, fuel, expense and document records together. Track odometer readings, running costs and full-tank fuel economy. Add optional photos and due dates, with local reminders when notification permission is enabled. Your private logbook is separate from your community-facing vehicle profile.

PEOPLE WHO SHARE YOUR PASSION
Share builds, photos, videos and ownership stories. Join communities, follow pages, message members and choose the audience for your posts. Reporting and blocking controls help you manage interactions.

A PLACE TO DISCOVER
Browse member listings, save favorites and discuss offers or counter-offers. Price insight uses Carbook listing data only; it is not an independent appraisal. Carbook does not process marketplace payments or guarantee sellers or vehicles.

MEETUPS, MAPPED
See pinned community events, open directions in your maps app and check in during the event window. Maps show event locations, not background tracking of your phone.

OPTIONAL TOOLS
Decode a VIN with NHTSA vPIC. Where configured by the community operator, ask an AI assistant about maintenance and ownership. AI can be wrong and is not a substitute for a qualified mechanic or inspection. Selecting vehicle context shares limited garage information with the operator's AI provider.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
Choose audiences and messaging preferences, export your information, or request account deletion in Settings & privacy. Carbook uses account-based access; messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

A configured Carbook service and sign-in account are required. Some communities may require an invitation. Remote notifications and AI depend on operator setup. Network access is needed for live content; a recent offline snapshot may be available on your device. Reminders depend on device settings and are not guaranteed.
