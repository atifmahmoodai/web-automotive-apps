# Content rating and app-content notes — draft

Do not guess an IARC rating or label this “Everyone”. Complete the live questionnaire for each distribution region after the configured service and policy are finalized.

- Primary app: automotive community/social networking; user-to-user interaction, private messaging, public/limited-audience posts, images/videos, marketplace listings and meetup coordination.
- User-generated content and information exchange: **Yes**. Members can share media and text. User content may differ from the app's own built-in content; answer each live questionnaire question as worded.
- User blocking and content/user reporting are implemented; authorized moderation and audit tools exist. These are not a substitute for a staffed moderation process, response targets and appeals.
- No built-in gambling, simulated gambling, randomized purchases, gambling ads, violence, sexual content, controlled-substance sales or paid checkout is deliberately implemented. Users may attempt prohibited content; moderation must enforce rules.
- Physical meetup locations and public check-ins are displayed, not background sharing of device GPS. Clearly disclose the user-visible attendance/location behavior.
- AI is configurable general automotive guidance, not certified advice. It needs a safety/abuse evaluation and provider policy review before activation. Determine whether Google Play's AI-generated-content reporting policy applies and implement an appropriate in-app flag/report path if required; do not assume ordinary post reporting covers AI output.
- Intended draft product policy is adults 18+, but no DOB/age-verification gate is currently implemented. Operator must choose target-audience age bands, implement appropriate access controls, and separately answer IARC honestly. A chosen 18+ policy is NOT an assigned IARC rating.
- Social apps may be subject to Play child-safety standards even when intended for adults: publish CSAE prohibition/standards, establish safety contact, user feedback mechanism, illegal-content escalation/reporting procedure and ensure required certification. Legal/safety operator review required.
- No ads, subscriptions or IAP currently implemented. “Ready to sell” does not mean a monetization system exists. A later digital subscription needs pricing, cancellation/refund terms and compliant store billing before advertising paid access.
- Final screenshot set must show real app behavior, not unsupported guarantees about valuations, verified sellers, vehicle inspections, AI, reminders or privacy.
