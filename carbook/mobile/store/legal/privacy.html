Information we process Sign-in uses an external OpenID Connect provider. The mobile service verifies the provider subject, display name and access-token claims; it stores a hash of issuer/subject mapped to your Carbook profile. The app requests openid, profile, email, offline_access and carbook scopes. The current mobile API does not persist an email claim; the identity provider may hold your email and other signup information under its own policy. Refresh credentials are held in device secure storage; access tokens are held in memory.

You choose your display name, bio, city, avatar, cover image, website and audience preferences. We store your posts, comments, reactions, polls, stories, follows, friendships, group/page activity, messages and attachments, reports, blocks, marketplace listings/offers and event attendance/check-ins. Community content is visible according to its audience and membership rules. Messages are participant-restricted, not end-to-end encrypted. Moderation reports can expose relevant content to authorized moderators.

Your garage stores vehicle name, year, notes and photos, and private service, fuel, expense and document records: titles, dates, amounts/currency, odometer, litres, due dates and optional receipt/document images. Public vehicle profiles and private logbook entries have different access rules. Do not upload identity documents or information you do not want processed. Selected photos/videos are uploaded only when you choose them; camera/library access is optional. No microphone recording, contacts access or device-GPS permission is requested by this build.

When you opt into notifications, Expo push tokens are associated with your account/device. Push notices can show names and activity summaries on your lock screen; direct-message notices do not contain message text. Vehicle reminders are scheduled locally and may show your car and record titles. App sign-out removes scheduled garage reminders and local account cache; remote push unregister is best effort when offline. Disable lock-screen previews in device settings if required.

External services and optional features Hosting/storage: [HOSTING PROVIDER AND REGIONS; intended Cloudflare Workers, D1 and R2]. Identity: [OIDC PROVIDER AND POLICY URL]. Push: Expo and platform delivery providers, if configured. These providers may process IP addresses and technical metadata.

VIN decoding sends the VIN you enter directly to the US NHTSA vPIC service. Map tiles reveal requested map areas and your IP address to the configured tile provider (default OpenStreetMap). Typed place searches go to the configured geocoder (default Nominatim); opening directions hands the chosen destination to your maps app. This is not background device-location tracking. You can avoid these optional features.

The optional AI assistant sends up to 12 conversation turns (bounded per turn) to [AI PROVIDER, REGION AND POLICY URL]. Selecting a car additionally sends its name, year, up to 400 characters of owner notes, and up to six recent service/fuel record titles, dates and odometer readings. The Carbook API does not store assistant conversation text; the chosen AI provider may log/retain it under its contract. Do not submit sensitive information. AI is not a mechanic and may be wrong. The assistant is unavailable until an operator configures it.

Optional crash and analytics reporting is OFF in this preview. If enabled in a future build, the app sends scrubbed error/stack information, app version/platform and limited event properties to operator-configured HTTPS endpoints. Network metadata may still identify a device. Update this policy and store disclosures before enabling it. Hosting access logs contain request time, method, path, status, duration and correlation ID; they do not intentionally log message bodies or bearer credentials. Infrastructure can separately log IP addresses.

Purposes and disclosure We use data to authenticate, provide community/garage/messaging/marketplace/event features, enforce safety rules, troubleshoot failures and prevent abuse. [ADD APPLICABLE LEGAL BASES, CROSS-BORDER TRANSFER SAFEGUARDS AND REGIONAL RIGHTS]. We do not currently run advertising, sell personal data, process payments or track people across other apps. Content you publish is shared with its intended audience. Operator-controlled processors and external services are listed above; do not interpret this draft as a finalized Google Play sharing classification.

Retention and deletion In Settings & privacy you can export information and request account deletion by typing DELETE MY ACCOUNT. Your profile is hidden immediately. There is a seven-day cancellation period, then background jobs remove uploaded media and authored content, blank your sent messages and anonymize the profile. Purging is batched (at most 100 media objects per scheduled pass; intended cron every 15 minutes) and can take longer for large accounts or outages. It is not instantaneous. An owner must transfer community ownership first; support must provide an assisted path if no eligible successor exists.

The current code retains a pseudonymous identity mapping/profile tombstone, conversation relationships, deletion status and audit metadata to prevent recreation/abuse and preserve integrity; audit detail for the deleted account is blanked. These retained records can remain linkable identifiers: they are not fully anonymous. Existing logs, infrastructure backups and third-party records are not purged by the account job. [SET JUSTIFIED MAXIMUM RETENTION WINDOWS, BACKUP EXPIRY AND PROVIDER DELETION PROCESS BEFORE LAUNCH]. User copies/screenshots cannot be recalled. Deleting Carbook does not delete an identity-provider account. Local offline snapshots expire after 14 days and are cleared on sign-out; Android backup is disabled.

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app using the account-deletion page and [VERIFIED SUPPORT CONTACT]. That channel is not configured in this draft. We may verify account ownership, but will never ask for your password or access token. See the deletion page for exactly what is removed/retained.
